AI & LLM evaluation

Training and evaluating large language models

Since September 2025 I have worked with Mercor, Turing, micro1 and Handshake AI on data and benchmarks that train and test model reasoning in mathematics and scientific computing.

RLHF & evaluation

Ranked, graded and critiqued model responses against detailed rubrics to produce RLHF and reward-model preference data; evaluated outputs across model families for correctness, reasoning depth and hallucinations.

Benchmark design

Designed 100+ high-difficulty mathematics and numerical-methods tasks with rigorous reference solutions, revised through 3–4 reviewer cycles for exact-match scoring.

SciCode & inverse problems

Built SciCode-style multi-step Python benchmarks delivered through GitHub repositories, including WENO reconstruction and inverse-problem tasks that probe ill-posedness and stability.
