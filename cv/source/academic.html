Research Profile

Applied mathematician and atmospheric scientist working on the diagnosis and numerical simulation of extreme precipitation over the Indian region: Himalayan cloudbursts, monsoon low-pressure systems and tropical cyclones. The work combines WRF/WRFDA modeling, 3DVAR data assimilation and reanalysis and satellite datasets (ERA5, IMDAA, IMD, GPM, INSAT), carried out in collaboration with scientists at IMD, NCMRWF and IITM.
